The direction remains aligned with [the vision](../vision.md). The new issues
preserve the separate graphics owner, private native boundaries, explicit
retirement evidence, bounded recovery, package-owned tests and platform consent.

replacement was prohibited by the bridge's initial-construction restriction,

The two new merged implementations are coherent with their accepted specs.
Focused logging and workflow checks passed. A targeted follow-up reproduced a
pre-existing X11 helper diagnostic race; this is a small workflow repair, not a

## Findings

### [#216] GUIDE-1. Admit replacement surfaces under the existing attachment

> **Disposition:** Owner-approved amendment posted to
> [#216](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/216#issuecomment-5755281107),
> with [coordination on #229](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/229#issuecomment-5755286919).
> The tracker disposition is complete; implementation remains in those issues.

**Disposition:** issue amendment to [#216](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/216),
coordinated with [#229](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/229).
**Verification:** verified conflict between effective specifications.
**Principle:** V-3/V-6; Vulkan D-24 and P-7.

**Evidence.** #216 requirement 5 permits creation only inside the registered
attachment's construction step. Its acceptance explicitly rejects creation
outside that step. #229 requirement 2 needs a new surface on the same live
window under the existing attachment, which must never be released or reattached.
Its placement paragraph explicitly calls #216 for that operation. Neither
issue's review amendments widen the bridge's eligibility. The accepted
[design P-7 at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/9f72b04e5ec876592fbe778592a66ed2578b15f6/docs/vulkan_backend_design.md#p-7-establish-one-loader-and-a-narrow-surface-bridge)
requires protection by the attachment, without restricting it to initial
attachment construction.

**Consequence.** A literal #216 solution would correctly pass its negative test
and later reject #229's required recovery call. A solver would then have to
widen a supposedly settled lifetime boundary or detach/re-attach against the
approved policy.

**Action.** Amend #216's eligibility and headless proof now; retain #229 as the
owner of recovery policy. No new issue, public native pointer or general-purpose
resource mechanism is needed. Tracker search found the recovery work in #229,
not an existing amendment that resolves this restriction.

**Approved amendment posted to #216:**

> Amendment to requirement 5 and its headless acceptance: “under the protected
> attachment” covers both initial attachment construction and an explicitly
> admitted replacement-surface operation on that same still-live attachment.
> Initial construction must not be the only phase in which the bridge can run.
>
> Bind each operation to the exact window, attachment and instance. Reserve its
> bounded handoff/rollback record before the native call, retain those parents
> until ownership transfer or verified cleanup, and serialize against terminal
> attachment/instance teardown. Keep native access private and creation on the
> main thread. Refuse foreign, stale, closing or otherwise unadmitted requests
> before a native effect; if close or cancellation follows creation, retain and
> deliver the owned cleanup obligation rather than publishing a usable target.
>
> Replace the initial-construction-only negative test with tests that permit
> an admitted replacement on an existing live attachment, reject unauthorized
> creation, and preserve the holds under close, cancellation and failed cleanup.
> VK-14/#229 still owns when recovery is admissible, old-generation and surface
> retirement order, and retry budgets; do not implement its recovery policy here.

### [#220] GUIDE-2. Assign required execution of Vulkan-linked headless suites

> **Disposition:** Owner-approved amendment posted to
> [#220](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/220#issuecomment-5755281785),
> with [shader-route coordination on #221](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/221#issuecomment-5755287054).
> The amendment names `test.vulkan-headless`, requires actual execution on a
> provisioned CPU worker without a native session, and keeps shader coverage
> continuous whichever issue lands first. Implementation remains in those issues.

**Disposition:** issue amendment to [#220](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/220).
**Verification:** verified delivery-plan gap, not a failing current native backend.
**Principle:** V-10/V-11; Vulkan D-21's required deterministic contracts.

**Evidence.** #219 introduces headless backend/controller examples through
`cabal.project.vulkan`; #222/#223/#225/#227/#229/#231 add lifecycle and failure
examples to those suites. Their acceptance requires local commands, but that
does not register future affected CI execution. #220 explicitly registers the
display group `test.vulkan-native`, keeps the CPU model group `test.vulkan`,
and migrates the proof route's *native* cases before removing the temporary
harness. Its effective spec does not assign execution of the Vulkan-linked
headless suites. #217 separately owns header-free capture tests; #221 explicitly
runs its shader suite through the proof/preparation path. Neither covers the
backend/controller suites.

The present
[catalog](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/9f72b04e5ec876592fbe778592a66ed2578b15f6/tools/validation/catalog.json)
has only the binding-free model group for Vulkan. Default/CPU projects deliberately
exclude the Vulkan binding. Building a package during preparation does not run
its headless suite. #220's amendment to
[proof-boundary checks](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/9f72b04e5ec876592fbe778592a66ed2578b15f6/tools/test/VulkanProof.hs#L90)
must allow the required headless route as well as the display route.

**Consequence.** Later lifecycle regressions can pass affected CI if the solver
runs the headless examples once but the delivered catalog only continues the
small real-device profile. Mixing those examples implicitly into native execution
also consumes the under-30-second native budget and makes them require a desktop.

**Action.** Have #220 explicitly own an affected/requested, non-optional
Vulkan-linked headless group (for example `test.vulkan-headless`) through
`cabal.project.vulkan`, on a provisioned worker with no display/session
acquisition. Keep the SDK-free model and capture groups unchanged. This group
may require headers and libraries to build; headless does not mean SDK-free.
It runs package-owned backend/controller suites and preserves #221's shader
contract execution through the migration. Future slices extend its inputs and
commands. Required deterministic examples do not move into optional probes.

Acceptance should prove that relevant input changes select this group, its
examples actually execute, a failure fails the receipt/check, and no desktop
consent is manufactured. Amend the old proof-boundary assertions accordingly.
Keep native fixture timing separate and account for compilation/preparation
without mistaking compilation for testing. These requirements are included in
the posted amendment; #221's coordination comment introduces no new dependency
between the shader and fixture slices.

### [#234] GUIDE-3. Distinguish X11 startup-channel failure from timeout

**Disposition:** small repair issue; independent of Vulkan/Wayland architecture.
**Verification:** reproduced at the pinned master.
**Principle:** V-10/V-11; reliable required tests and truthful failure diagnostics.

**Evidence.** PR #214 disclosed an existing X11 early-exit flake. This run
reproduced it in the existing Hspec example using stub display programs:
5 failures in 40 fresh processes with system Bash first on PATH, versus 0/40
with Homebrew Bash first. Each failure completed in roughly 0.03 seconds yet
said the server had not reported a display “within 30 seconds.”
The complete workflow suite happened to pass on this run.

In
[tools/display/x11.sh:98](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/9f72b04e5ec876592fbe778592a66ed2578b15f6/tools/display/x11.sh#L98),
the timed `read` outcome is discarded. On an empty result, a separate job-table
query chooses early-exit versus timeout. EOF and the job table's observation of
child exit need not arrive together. Thus an early channel close can take the
timeout branch without a timeout. The assertion at
[tools/test/Display.hs:150](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/9f72b04e5ec876592fbe778592a66ed2578b15f6/tools/test/Display.hs#L150)
then fails on the wrong diagnosis. No server or compositor was actually launched
by these examples.

**Consequence.** Required workflow tests can fail intermittently on a supported
local shell; users receive misleading startup diagnostics. Refusal itself is
safe: the wrapped command still does not run. This predates #214; the new
evidence, not a claim that Wayland introduced it, warrants reopening this path.

**Action.** Draft one small bug/test repair: distinguish timeout, EOF/invalid
startup report and observed child termination without depending on a race to
reap the child. Retain cleanup and refusal guarantees. Add coordinated stub
coverage for the report channel closing before child-exit observation and for
a genuine timeout; do not merely accept any error string or add sleeps until
the flaky assertion happens to pass. Keep default startup bounds and caller
consent behavior unchanged.

Exact reproduction selection:
`/Isolated X11 display/refuses to run the command when the X server exits before reporting a display/`.
Run the already built workflow executable repeatedly with that Hspec
`--match` selection and `/usr/bin:/bin:/usr/sbin:/sbin` first on PATH.
Retained records: `x11-early-exit-system-bash.json` and
`x11-early-exit-repeats.json` in this run's local evidence directory.
Searches of open/closed issues and PRs found the disclosure in #214 and the
older fixture issue, but no open repair owning this race.
