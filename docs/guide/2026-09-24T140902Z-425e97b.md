## Findings

### [#253] GUIDE-1. Preserve the primary body failure when diagnostic finalization is cancelled

> **Disposition, 2026-09-24:** Filed as [#253](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/issues/253) after owner approval. Creation does not establish readiness or implementation.

**Disposition:** repair. **Verification:** reproduced against the pinned code.
**Principle:** V-3/V-4; `withDiagnosticCapture` promises the body's original type,
value and context with a diagnostic verdict attached. #217 requires primary
failure preservation; foundation and protected-host cleanup establish the same
precedence.

**Evidence:**
[Diagnostics.hs:391](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/425e97b553f28addab1b73c7d5ba39dcbc36b3a9/packages/gpu-vulkan/diagnostics/src/Hetoimasia/GPU/Vulkan/Diagnostics.hs#L391)
catches the body failure as a value before finalization.
[The final selection at line 431](https://github.com/coghex/hetoimasia/blob/425e97b553f28addab1b73c7d5ba39dcbc36b3a9/packages/gpu-vulkan/diagnostics/src/Hetoimasia/GPU/Vulkan/Diagnostics.hs#L431)
then prefers `finishedPending` and the enclosing group's late failure over
`finishedOutcome`. Consequently the worker group cannot preserve the body's
primary on behalf of this wrapper: it never received that exception as its
body's failure.

A standalone program used the package's existing `gatedLogger`, `awaitInSink`
and `awaitPhase` helpers, with the production capture and C producer. It:

1. Offered one warning and waited until the drain worker entered the gated sink.
2. Recorded quiescence with `afterLastCallback capture (pure ())` (there were
   no native objects), then threw a typed `PrimaryFailure` from the body.
3. Waited for `PhaseClosed`, proving the body had failed and finalization was
   waiting on the worker.
4. In the control, opened the sink gate; in the failing case, delivered
   `ThreadKilled` to the capture's caller instead.
5. Inspected the propagated exception after the worker had settled.

```text
cancel=False primaryPreserved=True exception=PrimaryFailure
cancel=True primaryPreserved=False exception=thread killed
```

The assertion that both preserve `PrimaryFailure` exits unsuccessfully. The
interleaving uses STM/MVars, not sleeps; the bounded waits only prevent a hung
reproducer. Source, compile log and result are retained locally under
`.git/guide-evidence/20260924T140902Z-425e97b/diagnostic-primary-repro/`
and its parent. This report's sequence and pinned call sites preserve the
reproduction contract if those scratch artifacts are later removed.

**Consequence:** shutdown cancellation can hide the actual application/native
failure that began unwinding. Existing examples cover body failure plus sink
failure, and cancellation during finalization after a successful body,
separately. All 56 pass; neither case covers their relevant combination.

**Smallest repair:** settle the original body failure first, preserving its type,
value and existing context; retain later finalization/cancellation evidence
alongside the verdict. When the body succeeded, keep cancellation observable.
Cover the combined failure path with an explicitly coordinated regression and
retain the successful-body cancellation case. Reconcile the finalization
precedence prose in the owning module and `docs/vulkan_diagnostics.md` in the
same repair PR. Inspect both the direct finalization cancellation and the late
worker-group exit branch; do not merely discard later failures.

**Deduplication:** all open issue titles and the captured historical tracker
were checked for diagnostic/primary/cancellation/cleanup work. Closed #217 is
the delivered source of this bug; #250 owns names/labels, #251 owns read-only
worker observation, and #231 owns later backend terminal teardown. None owns
this wrapper's exception selection. No new issue was drafted or filed.
