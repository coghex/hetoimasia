## 1. Measurement freshness

### [#373] PRR-1. Invalidate probe observations when their execution policy changes

> **Captured note:** P2 — Include the policy that determines a suite's build
> and execution in its source identity. PR #322 derives preparation, timeout,
> platform and display routing from the catalog and CI workflow, but its digest
> does not cover those policy values for most suites. A changed probe can be
> skipped as unchanged using an observation made under different conditions.

**Verification:** At the verification pin, a temporary directory overlay
symlinked to the pinned checkout held a separate copy of `catalog.json`; the
review tree and personal configuration were untouched. For `test.x11-helper`,
changing `timeout_seconds` from 600 to 300 and adding a schema-valid preparation
command that builds the component with `--ghc-options=-O0` produced a suite
with the new 300-second timeout and the new preparation, but exactly the same
identity as the original suite. The planner's preparation validation returned
no problems. Passing that changed suite and a synthetic recent observation of
the original identity to the actual quruntul `test_order` selected nothing and
reported `unchanged and within 7 days`. This proves a missed changed-probe
selection, without running the long probe, compiling a modified build, or
writing a measurement ledger.

**Evidence:**

- `.quruntul/adapter.py:115` — the catalog is read separately from the
  component-source entries used for the digest; workflow parsing also supplies
  routing and display policy.
- `.quruntul/adapter.py:143` — digest inputs include component inputs, the
  group's source patterns and selected project/toolchain files, but do not
  universally include catalog or workflow policy. `test.x11-helper` does not
  declare either policy file as an input.
- `.quruntul/adapter.py:146` — the digest covers adapter bytes, component,
  selector options and matching source entries. It omits the other consumed
  group/routing values.
- `.quruntul/adapter.py:161` and `:167` — timeout and preparation nevertheless
  change the emitted suite and the build that `prepare` will run.
- `docs/test_classification.md:137` — `$test` should select a probe changed
  since its last run, independently of the weekly stale-observation rule.
- External `quruntul/select.py:76–82` at the named engine revision — only a
  changed identity makes a recently observed probe due; an equal identity is
  skipped until stale. `quruntul/adapter.py:37` defines the identity as the
  digest of every input that can change suite behaviour.

**Handoff context:**

- **Current behavior:** A catalog-only change to a probe's build preparation
  or execution deadline can reuse its old identity. Automatic `$test`
  selection defers the changed workload until its old observation becomes
  stale. Analogous omitted routing and platform inputs can leave flake
  enumeration unaware that previously pending cases have changed conditions.
- **Expected behavior:** A change to policy that materially changes the suite's
  build, execution or applicable environment changes its identity, so freshness
  is assessed against the actual measured conditions.
- **Scope and constraints:** Correct this repository's adapter identity
  boundary. Preserve catalog authority, deterministic identities, exact
  per-test profile selection, preparation commands, platform/desktop policy,
  and the owner's rule that stable examples are not automatically remeasured.
  Do not change quruntul's selector or hand-edit its ledger. Hashing relevant
  policy values or policy sources are possible approaches; no particular
  representation is required.
- **Verification target:** Against temporary policy overlays, prove a
  preparation or execution-policy change alters the affected suite identity
  and makes a recently observed changed probe selectable. Keep unchanged
  identities stable and distinct, and retain the existing adapter checks.
  Exercise relevant routing/platform changes too. No desktop or real
  measurement ledger is needed for this boundary check.
- **Deduplication:** The claim-held #322 row has no earlier report/history.
  Complete open-issue inventory and all-state searches for `quruntul identity`
  and `adapter catalog` found no matching issue. Related planner refactor #324
  and Wayland qualification #327 do not specify this freshness correction.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** The experiment proves the selection error under
  valid policy changes, not a count of already missed runs in the owner's
  ledger. No actual measurement ledger was inspected or modified.
