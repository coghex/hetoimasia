## Proposal · ownership

Keep the existing scoped worker, stage types, scene ledger, runtime owner and graphics owner. A resource has one disposal obligation even when several parties have interests in it. Every transition below removes the obligation from its source and installs it at its destination in the same STM commit. For an IO acquisition, use the [resource contract](../resources.md#mask-discipline) to protect the partial result until registration. No interruptible step or hook may intervene. If an IO action can acquire before throwing, its provider must register the acquired prefix. A failed transfer leaves the source responsible. An uncertain disposal becomes a charged failure entry with its identity and cause. Continue independent cleanup and preserve primary and secondary failures.

| Resource | Owner and transfer transaction | Disposal or proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Style, texture interests and CPU coverage | Caller acquires the checked immutable style parents under protection. Enqueue transfers them to the request. Dequeue transfers them to the protected worker. Opening the runtime view adds it to the same rollback scope. Attach commit validates host, lease/client, palette, depth, capacities, live original handles and the pending box. It takes the claim, retains one GPU ledger interest per distinct texture, installs the view and settles the box together. | A cancellation racing commit has one winner. Rollback closes the runtime view and drops each acquired parent. On success the view keeps its own authority after the caller releases a handle. Coverage stays charged once per exact asset version through the last CPU reader. |
| Claim and runtime demand | The session's claim table grants one publisher per target incarnation. A live view owns its runtime view and latest demand. An intent commit replaces only this view's intent and settles superseded jumps once. | Close admission first, clear its demand and close its runtime view. Hand back the matching claim as below. The arena, target and peer views stay with their owners. |
| Regional request, borrow and copied inputs | Record the pending handle in the copying stage at issue. In the take transaction, transfer the snapshot to a funded stage and return the runtime borrow, or discard the snapshot and return the borrow. Abandonment moves the pending handle to service disposal in the stage-reset transaction. | Keep the entry until cancellation wins or the settlement and borrow release commit together. If cancellation loses, take and release the borrow exactly once. A throwing `cancelling` hook still runs this disposal on an unconditional path. Other pending entries remain discoverable. |
| Candidate, build and GPU reservation | The worker reserves CPU/GPU before making inputs or submitting a build. Submission installs its ticket with the reservation in the stage. On the graphics thread, record each registered chunk under the acquisition mask. Supersession transfers ticket, byte counts and registered prefix to `svOrphans`, or transfers finished fresh chunks to retirement. Reused chunks stay with their published owner until commit. | A collected ticket transfers its built prefix to upload or retirement and refunds only the proved unbuilt suffix. A failed upload retires the affected build and leaves the previous publication coherent. |
| Accepted scenes, chunks and retirements | Force the whole scene before publication. The acceptance STM rechecks claim, authority, intent, arena mark, metrics, generations and limits, installs the host's real barrier protection, transfers CPU data to the revision pin, and transfers replaced regions **with their charges** to retirement. Every adoption adds a host ledger hold, including repeated adoptions. | Never-published chunks skip the publication barrier. Published chunks need a met barrier, zero adoption holds and settled upload before owner release. Each chunk's positive release answer refunds its own CPU/GPU charges. Failed or unanswered release retains that chunk's evidence and credit. A peer can still retire. |
| Plan pins and readers | The view owns a pin per protected revision. Acquiring a reader increments that revision's count in the same STM that selects the adopted plan. Pruning keeps published, still-adoptable, adopted and read revisions. On close, reader-held pins and their style interest pass to the last-reader cleanup. | Close can hand the target back immediately. Each reader releases under protection on every exit. Final release removes the backing references and refunds the applicable credit in one transition. A retained closed handle exposes only terminal metadata. |

## Proposal · what may wake the worker

The following are the complete **external** wait inputs. Resolve each cell once for its owner or incarnation. Its producer writes it in the transaction changing the named value, on change only. A projection of a frequently written TVar still subscribes STM to every write.

| Selection condition | Cells allowed in the blocking read set |
| --- | --- |
| Stop or check authority, from **every** stage | Service-open latch, session admission latch, graphics-owner ended/failed latch, exact target retirement latch, terrain `authorityEpochSTM`. On epoch change schedule a one-shot lease check and record the epoch with the check. Resolve `arenaMarkSTM` at attach and watch the returned per-arena cell for content, knowledge, residency or end. |
| Advance or supersede a view | That target's effective metrics cell, that target's newest adoption, the pending copy's settlement box, or the submitted build's answer. Metrics change only on relevant metric changes. Pin pressure waits for capacity computed from **which revisions** remain protected. |
| Capacity refused | Read only the reason for this refusal: available shared account bytes against the required rounded delta, GPU bytes against required size, retained-bank room, or the runtime admission capacity that actually refused the copy/demand. Give `retainedBankUseSTM` its own change-only cell in `putSt`. For upload pressure use a settled-upload counter captured with build submission. For a build without a ticket use ordinary action room including device readiness. |
| Upload or retirement | Wait on the named chunk's upload standing or orphan's ticket. `RetireUnasked` alone needs release-lane room. `RetireWaiting` reads its barrier answer. `RetireMet` examines that chunk's upload and last adoption hold, then ordinary room if release is eligible. `RetireReleasing` reads its release ticket. An empty list subscribes to neither queue. |

Local control inputs are request/cleanup queue membership, live-view membership, close request, stage and its saved wait reason, latest intent, prepared/published identity, continuation and demand-pending flags, pin membership and per-revision reader occupancy. The round-robin cursor is worker-owned bookkeeping. Diagnostics, step counts and generic release counts are never wake conditions. Do one bounded step, prioritizing stop, close and eligible cleanup, then rotate runnable ordinary work so incoming attaches cannot indefinitely displace an existing view.

Obtain actual action/release room and owner end from the host's admission state. Expose change-only projections for room, device readiness, settlement, target adoption and per-chunk hold/standing where the existing accessor reaches a shared registry. Remove a per-key observation when its last subscriber/owner leaves. The blocking selector must never read runtime `serviceState`, per-frame graphics state, scene/target tables, whole upload/retained registries, or probe by attempting publication, submission, release or a destructive take. Read full state for authoritative **nonwaiting** commits. Install the relevant wait predicate with the refusal, or capture its monotone event counter before the attempt and recheck immediately. A capacity already sufficient proceeds without waiting for a second edge.

## Proposal · terminal outcomes

Store one write-once result per view. Preserve the `closeView ∷ … → IO ()` success path. All present and later close waiters read the same result and receive a typed failure if close failed. Distinguish the reason admission ended from whether handback succeeded. Closing precludes new reads and intents and settles pending jumps cancelled. An already-settled operation keeps its answer.

| Cause | Cleanup and evidence | Waiter outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit close | If the matching claim published to a live target, publish the initial empty content as a **new revision** and release the claim in one STM. An unpublished claim releases without publishing. A replaced claim is never cleared. Transfer independent obligations as above. | Close succeeds after logical handback and transfer to identified cleanup owners. GPU retirement remains observable separately. |
| Lease, client or arena revoked | Mark closing in any stage, dispose the runtime view, hand back a still-live matching target and record the revocation. | View ends revoked. Close waiters receive the handback outcome. |
| Target retired | Confirm the exact incarnation ended, release its claim without publishing, retain real adoption and batch holds. | View ends target-gone. Close completes on logical disposal. |
| Graphics owner ends or fails while session still takes work | Stop admission immediately. Collect answered tickets/prefixes first. Release independent runtime, style and CPU obligations. Record each unproved claim, build, chunk and charge with its last known state. | Queued attaches settle stopped, jumps cancelled, live views observe owner end without waiting for scope exit. Close reports failure wherever safe handback cannot be proved. |
| Session admission closes, scope ends, or worker exits | Stop new work, settle queued attaches, drive close/disposal for every view, settle or transfer retirements, signal worker completion on every exit and join before dependencies unwind. Retain worker or cleanup failure. | No queued attach or close waits for a dead worker. A scope failure/cancellation propagates with cleanup evidence. |
| Hook throws or close handback refuses/throws | Run independent releases even if the hook fails. Retain its failure and always signal waiters. For stage or attach hooks, use the committed stage or settlement box to determine which owner cleans up. Inside handback, a false publication with a still-live matching claim is a failure. Roll back claim release, retain the claim and dependencies of the still-current scene in a service-owned failure entry, and detach independent resources in separate commits. | Close records failed handback once. No synthetic success, repeat publication or retry as pressure. A post-handback hook failure records that handback actually committed and reports its own failure. |

Quiescence and host progress run while dependencies can still answer. The uninterruptible release path may join controlled worker cleanup. It must never await GPU completion, target adoption, an unbounded hook or a user reader under a publication lock or an uninterruptible lease finalizer. If host proof is outstanding at view-scope exit, atomically move the ticket, refs, charges and disposition to the enclosing graphics session before signalling the join. Its owner services these entries through the existing release lane while running and accounts for unresolved entries at its own end. Never implement a device wait indirectly by joining a worker blocked on that device.

## Proposal · public values, costs and construction

Only opaque lifetime handles, forced compact readings, settlement outcomes and `PickNoHit`, `PickLoading` or `PickHit` escape. Compute the pick and force all returned fields while its adopted pin is protected, including the cell, part and identity. A thunk selecting a field from the plan keeps the plan alive. Full-plan access stays in private qualification code with a scoped reader whose result cannot export backing storage. A precondition retains only small validity cells and identity data. Charge each reader's metadata and charge the shared revision/coverage backing once until its last required hold ends.

Use one set of obligation sizes for exact admission and its published bounds. Sum each snapshot's footprint, fixed inputs for distinct regions, expanded knowledge by column/run, and removal-index column and **clipped interval occurrences per snapshot**. Add the same first-quantum allowance for `preparationCost`; include granule rounding when advertising account room. For an upper bound, sum over a worst permitted partition into snapshots, including per-copy overhead. With 256 columns and 32 levels per region there are at most 16 disjoint nonadjacent runs per column per region. A safe per-region cap is therefore 256 column entries plus 4,096 interval entries, summed once per copied region occurrence. This covers admission even before the merged change limit is checked. Apply a tighter cap only if its precondition is established before charging. Use checked wide arithmetic. With limit 1, one removed run crossing two vertical regions in separate snapshots costs 128 bytes before coalescing.

At construction reject an over-limit config before opening a worker or accepting a request. Eight live views per service, two protected CPU revisions per view, 128 MiB of terrain GPU data and 128 MiB in the **single injected** CPU render/staging account are hard maxima. Accept smaller supported settings, including zero GPU allowance. Preserve tighter texture, native depth, queue and recorder limits. Keep old and retiring data charged through proof of disposal.

## Proposal · identity at the point of use

Bind each production target to the issuing Vulkan host and exact registration/window incarnation. Derive the service's host identity from its actual ports. Reject a foreign target before acquiring even the caller-side style parents, then recheck registration and claim in the attach commit. A target callback validating its own host proves only half the relation.

Qualify the claim holder and world tag by the view service's opaque incarnation as well as the local view number, or allocate view IDs from the shared claim owner so simultaneous services cannot collide. A pick carries it, target/host, view, adopted revision/tag, arena and revisions. Bind the runtime precondition to the issuing terrain service's existing `serviceTag`. Preserve ordinary content-only preconditions. In final removal/settlement STM the recipient checks its own tag, lease and arena, then the pick's issuing view, current matching claim/adoption, content, knowledge, residency and metrics. Camera, settings and exact assets belong to the immutable adopted revision. A stale or foreign reference yields `StaleView` with no edit, including after storage preparation. Equality of local counters or a successful guard run against the issuer cannot authorize the recipient.
